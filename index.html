Industrial Workflow

Our 6-Stage Manufacturing Process

From raw potato selection to export packaging, each step is engineered for high-grade frozen output.

01

Potato Selection

Careful inspection for high solid content, low sugar levels, and uniform size.

02

Preparation & Peeling

Gentle washing, steam/mechanical peeling, and inspection sorting.

03

Precision Cutting

High-speed water-gun or blade cutting into exact 7mm, 9mm, or 12mm profiles.

04

Controlled Par-Frying

Brief par-frying in vegetable oil to seal outer texture while keeping center light.

05

IQF Freezing

Rapid individual freezing tunnel down to -18°C to lock in freshness instantly.

06

Export Packaging

Automated weight dispensing, sealed protective bags, and corrugated export cartons.
